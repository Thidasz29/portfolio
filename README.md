# Thidas Perera portfolio (GitHub Pages)

## Put it online
1. Create a new public repository on github.com (for example `portfolio`).
2. Click **Add file > Upload files**, drag in everything from this folder (`index.html`, `data.json`, `.nojekyll`, `README.md`) and commit.
3. Open **Settings > Pages**, set **Source** to *Deploy from a branch*, choose **main** and **/ (root)**, then Save.
4. After about a minute your site is live at `https://YOUR-USERNAME.github.io/portfolio/`.

## Edit your profile photo, details and achievements
1. Open your live site, scroll to the footer and click **Edit this page** (or add `?edit` to the address).
2. Click any dashed text to change it. Click your portrait or **Change photo** to upload a new one. Use **Add achievement** and **Delete** to manage achievements.
3. Your changes are saved in *this browser only*. To publish them for everyone, click **Export data.json**, then in your repository upload the new `data.json` over the old one and commit.
4. If your browser still shows old content after you change `data.json` on GitHub, click **Edit this page > Reset**.

Visitors can open edit mode too, but their changes stay in their own browser and never reach your site.
